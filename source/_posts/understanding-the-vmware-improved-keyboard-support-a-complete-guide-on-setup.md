### Step 2: Import your Google bookmarks into Firefox

1. Open Firefox.
2. Click the**Library** icon and click**Bookmarks** .  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/img_5bdbfc8da5140.png)
3. Click**Show All Bookmarks** .
