### **Way 2: Get a verification code by your phone number**

 If you’ve enabled the 2-step authentication with a phone number, you can recover your Gmail password by getting a verification code sent by Google to verify that it’s your account.

 Note: You should have your phone handy, or have**access** to the phone number to receive the**verification code** . If you lost that phone, firstly you should go to the carrier and have a new SIM card for that phone number.

 1) Go to[https://mail.google.com/](https://mail.google.com/) in your browser, and enter the Google **email address** that you want to recover.

 Or you can directly go to the[Gmail account recovery center](https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery) , enter your Gmail address,and click **NEXT** .
