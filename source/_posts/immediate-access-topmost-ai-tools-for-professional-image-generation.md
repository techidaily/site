##  Stable Diffusion

![Stable Diffusion "a robot eating a taco"](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/stable-diffusion.png) 

_Jason Fitzpatrick / Stable Diffusion_

[Stable Diffusion](https://extra-information.techidaily.com/5-highest-rated-vr-gear-for-uavs-for-2024/) is an open-source machine learning image generator. It can create images from text prompts like many other tools, plus modify existing images, upscale blurry images, and [generate video using an extension](https://youtube-help.techidaily.com/in-2024-sonic-gold-standard-10-ways-to-elevate-home-recordings/). Stable Diffusion runs locally on your own computer. We have a [handy guide for getting it up and running](https://unlock-android.techidaily.com/7-ways-to-unlock-a-locked-xiaomi-redmi-k70-phone-by-drfone-android/).

Related: [How to Run Stable Diffusion on Your PC to Generate AI Images](https://unlock-android.techidaily.com/7-ways-to-unlock-a-locked-xiaomi-redmi-k70-phone-by-drfone-android/) 

 The cool thing about Stable Diffusion is it's completely free, and you get a lot of control since it runs locally. You will need to make sure your computer meets the requirements, though.

 If you don't have the horsepower to run it on your system, you can also [try a demo of Stable Diffusion on the web](https://huggingface.co/spaces/stabilityai/stable-diffusion). There are also other websites hosting Stable Diffusion online. The Stable Diffusion subreddit has [a long list of places you can use it in your browser](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/wuyu2u/how%5Fdo%5Fi%5Frun%5Fstable%5Fdiffusion%5Fand%5Fsharing%5Ffaqs/).
