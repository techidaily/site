##  Bing Image Creator

![Boosts count.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2023-03-21_11-32-03.jpg) 

Joe Fedewa / How-To Geek

 Microsoft's Bing Image Creator is a DALL-E powered image generator. Think of it as the AI art equivalent to [Bing's AI Chat feature](https://fox-info.techidaily.com/2024-approved-journey-to-stickers-full-tutorial-for-turning-gifs-in-chat-apps-like-discord-and-telegram/).

 Unlike DALL-E, [Bing Image Creator](https://facebook-video-content.techidaily.com/2024-approved-social-networking-101-registering-with-facebook/) is completely free with no limits. You get 25 "boosts" to speed up image generation each week, but you can generate all the image you like. You just might have to wait a bit. You can access and use Bing Image Creator on the web at <https://www.bing.com/images/create>.

 It's simple and easy to use---all you need is a Microsoft account. Microsoft launched it on March 21, 2023, on the same day Google released [Google Bard](https://youtube-video-recordings.techidaily.com/in-2024-enhance-branding-the-ultimate-guide-to-custom-urls-for-youtube/).

##  Craiyon (DALL-E Mini)

![Craiyon "a robot eating a taco"](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2022-09-07_15-20-28.png) 

_Joe Fedewa / Craiyon_

 DALL-E Mini skyrocketed in popularity after DALL-E 2 started appearing in the news. It was later renamed to "Craiyon" to avoid confusion. [Craiyon is a web tool](https://www.craiyon.com/), and it's not nearly as impressive as the others. However, it's super easy to use with no accounts or anything else. You just enter a text prompt and let it do it's thing.

##  NightCafe

![NightCafe "a robot eating a taco"](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/nightcafe.png) 

_Joe Fedewa / NightCafe_

[NightCafe](https://www.googleadservices.com/pagead/aclk?sa=L&ai=DChcSEwiquty%5FmOr5AhUFkmYCHU6rDlwYABAAGgJzbQ&ae=2&ohost=www.google.com&cid=CAESbeD29ugk5wOBmcxKNDfI1cPqOXMu1p%5FDYEBXztalK%5Fe66QwYhRpAsWm8uy0XxIevH-rjvg5-diykYZ1%5FoKVq8FTtJ%5FkdHQsx6CZiwXZA81h5cYlmLrtsXBELyzZpUmnfvxIucRm9BrLp1XaTVRE&sig=AOD64%5F20TFQt3LJkcbDNk-GAwDu882Qwbg&q&adurl&ved=2ahUKEwiHqdW%5FmOr5AhU%5F7TgGHd0wDH4Q0Qx6BAgCEAE) is a web tool that creates images from text prompts in specific art styles. For example, you can choose styles such as "oil painting," "cubist," "fantasy," or "3D game." NightCafe also includes the ability to make videos, and it has a pretty decent community around it.

 You are limited to five free creations each day, and the process can be a bit slow due to heavy traffic. To create more than that, you'll need to purchase additional credit packs starting at $7.99.

##  Dream by Wombo

![Dream "a robot eating a taco"](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/dream.jpg) 

_Joe Fedewa / Dream_

[Dream by Wombo](https://app.wombo.art/) is a similar implementation to NightCafe. Text prompts are paired with art styles, such as "polygon," "realistic," "meme," and "line-art." However, you do have the option to choose "no style" if you'd like. Dream is accessed on the web, and there's a mobile version as well. Dream is completely free to use and there are no daily limitations.
