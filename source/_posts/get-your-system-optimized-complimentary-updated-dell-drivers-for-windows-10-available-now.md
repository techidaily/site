**How to Download Drivers from Dell**
  
Following steps are for your reference.  
  
1\. Go to[Dell driver download page](https://shop-links.co/link/?exclusive=1&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.dell.com%2Fsupport%2Fhome%2Fus%2Fen%2F19%2FProducts%2F%3Fapp%3Ddrivers).
2\. To find the right drivers, first, you need to identify your product. After entering the download page, as you can see, there are three ways for you to identify your Dell product.  
Choose the way that suits for you. (Here we will take “Browse for a product” to download Windows 7 drivers for “Dell Vostro 3900” for example.)  
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/img_5743c7c9e910d.png)
  
 If you don’t know your product model name, it is recommended that you click**Detect Product** . In this way, you are required to download and install Dell System Detect. After that, your product will be detected automatically.

3\. View products then select your product. For  Dell Vostro 3900, select**Desktops & All-in-ones** .
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/img_5743c9c8d4f02.png)
  
 4\. Select the Series. For  Dell Vostro 3900, select**Vostro** .
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/img_5743ca665b2a4.png)

5\. Select the specific product name.  
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/img_5743cb02886b7.png)

6\. Then you will be directed to the product download page. Click**Find it myself**tab and change the OS to the specific operating system that your PC is running (Windows 7 64-bit or Windows 7 32-bit.)  
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/img_5743cd44ee33b.png)
  
7\. Then you will see a list of drivers displayed. Download the driver you need. You may need to identify the correct version before downloading.
