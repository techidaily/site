The $900 price tag is a great value compared to many PCs as well, where towers can cost more, and it doesn't include a monitor. With the iMac, you get everything you need right out of the box, and all of the hardware is built into the monitor itself. And finally, it just looks fantastic—iMacs make for a color, yet sleek and clean looking workstation. It just can't get much better than this.
