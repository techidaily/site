 So, unless you're looking to do some heavy processing or high-end gaming, the M1 iMac is a fantastic purchase. The $900 price tag is a great value compared to many PCs as well, where towers can cost more, and it doesn't include a monitor. With the iMac, you get everything you need right out of the box, and all of the hardware is built into the monitor itself. And finally, it just looks fantastic—iMacs make for a color, yet sleek and clean looking workstation. It just can't get much better than this.

 It's hard to tell how long Best Buy will hold this particular sale, but it's best not to wait too long. If dropping $900 in one day isn't possible, you can sign up for a 12-month financing plan, so you can pay $75/month if you qualify.
