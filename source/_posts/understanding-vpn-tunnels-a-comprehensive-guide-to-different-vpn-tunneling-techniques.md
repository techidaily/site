### Tip 1: Choose a reputable Internet browser

 First things first, we recommend using a reputable Internet browser to ensure the basic security, like**Google Chrome** ,**Microsoft Edge** ,**Mozilla FireFox** ,**Apple Safari** and etc. These browsers can protect you from most phishing contents and malware.
