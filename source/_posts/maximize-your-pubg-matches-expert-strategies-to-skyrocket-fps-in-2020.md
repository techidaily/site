## Step 2: Download & Install Minecraft Forge

 Forge is a modding API that makes it easier to create and verify mods for Minecraft. So to download and install it, you can:

**Download**

 1) Navigate to the[Forge download page](http://files.minecraftforge.net/) and download the recommended version of Forge.
  
![Install Forge](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Install-Forge-1.jpg)

 You can also download the latest version of Forge. But it can have some unresolved bugs. And you’re supposed to install the version of Minecraft Forge that corresponds to the version of Minecraft you are playing.

 2) From the**Download Recommended** section, click**Installer** .
  
![Install Forge](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Install-Forge-2.jpg)

 3) There will be a line “**Please wait … seconds…** ”. So just wait for several seconds and don’t click anything.
  
![Install Forge](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Install-Forge-3.jpg)

 4) On the upper right corner of the page, click**Skip** .  
  
![Install Forge](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Install-Forge-4.jpg)

 5) Click**Save** when the**Save As** window pops up. And Forge will begin downloading.
  
![Install Forge](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Install-Forge-5.jpg)

**Install**

 1) Double-click the Forge file to open the program.  
 (You can open it on your web browser or in your Downloads folder.)

 2) Check**Install client** and click**OK** .  
  
![Install Forge](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Install-Forge-7.jpg)
  
 3) Simply click**OK** when the Complete window pops up and the installation process will be completed.
